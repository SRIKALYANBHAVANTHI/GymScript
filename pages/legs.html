Legs + Forearms + Abs Workout

Legs

  1. Barbell Back Squats – 3-4 sets
  2. Leg Extensions – 3 sets
  3. Seated Leg Curls – 3 sets
  4. Hip Thrusts / Hip Extensions – 3 sets
  5. Seated Calf Raises – 3 sets (15-20 reps)
  6. Standing Calf Raises – 3 sets (15-20 reps)

Forearms

  1. Wrist Curls – 3 sets
  2. Reverse Wrist Curls – 3 sets
  3. Farmer’s Carries (optional finisher) – 2 rounds

Abs

  1. Ab Machine Crunches – 3 sets
  2. Optional: Hanging Leg Raises / Cable Crunches – 2-3 sets
